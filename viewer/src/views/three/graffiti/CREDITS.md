# Graffiti references

The wall art (`wallArt.ts`, `painter.ts`, `glyphs.ts`) is drawn procedurally at runtime from
hand-authored vector letter skeletons. No photos ship with the app. The letter shapes and the
way the pieces are built up (3D block, outer ink line, cut-back, flat fill bands, white shines,
drips, arrows, bubble overlap, handstyle lean, buffs) were studied from these openly licensed
photos on Wikimedia Commons. Only generic letter construction was taken from them. Every word on
the wall is made up, and no writer's name, tag, crew, character or logo was copied.
